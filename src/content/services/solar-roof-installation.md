- **Single Contractor Responsibility:** No finger-pointing between roofers and solar installers. We warranty both the roof deck and the solar waterproofing penetrations.
- **Flawless Flashing & Sealing:** New roof underlayment and flashing brackets are installed simultaneously, guaranteeing zero water infiltration.
- **Streamlined Financing & Permitting:** One inspection, one permit process, and unified monthly financing options.
