The inverter is the beating heart of your solar system — converting DC electricity generated by your panels into AC electricity your home appliances use. When an inverter fails, your panels can generate power that never reaches your home.

## Rapid Troubleshooting & Replacement

Solar Pro technicians carry testing equipment to quickly isolate whether the problem is on the AC grid side, internal electronics, or DC string input.

- **Microinverter Replacements:** Fast replacement of single failed microinverters without taking down the rest of your solar array.
- **Hybrid Inverter Upgrades:** Transition from obsolete string inverters to battery-ready hybrid models with seamless backup switching.
- **Manufacturer Warranty Processing:** We help you navigate manufacturer RMA replacement claims for covered hardware.
