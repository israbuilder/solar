## Engineered for Texas Roofs

Every home has unique sun exposure, roof pitch, and energy usage patterns. Solar Pro never uses one-size-fits-all kits. We utilize high-resolution satellite imagery, drone lidar scans, and historical kilowatt-hour analysis to custom-engineer an array optimized for your property.

- **Tier-1 Monocrystalline Panels:** Maximum efficiency in both direct sun and diffuse cloud cover.
- **Microinverters & Smart Optimizers:** Ensure panel-level monitoring and prevent shade from reducing total system output.
- **Wind & Weather Rated Racking:** Engineered specifically for the Gulf Coast wind zone with sealed, leak-proof roof attachments.
