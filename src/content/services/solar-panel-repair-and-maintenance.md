Even the most robust solar energy systems can experience issues over time due to Texas weather, electrical component wear, or substandard original installation. Solar Pro provides diagnostic and repair services to bring your solar generation back to 100%.

## What We Diagnose & Fix

- **Inverter Failure & Error Codes:** Rapid diagnostics for string inverters, central inverters, and microinverter communication errors.
- **Storm & Hail Damage:** Detailed inspections, insurance claim documentation, and replacement of cracked or damaged modules.
- **Critter & Pest Damage:** Repairing chewed DC cabling and installing galvanized critter guards to prevent squirrels and birds from nesting under panels.
- **Roof Leak Repair Around Mounts:** Flashing remediation and waterproofing to correct leaks caused by previous incompetent installers.
