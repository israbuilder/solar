When extreme Texas weather strikes — from winter freezes to Gulf Coast hurricanes — the electrical grid can leave neighborhoods without power for days. Solar battery storage ensures your household remains safe, cool, and fully energized no matter what happens to the utility grid.

## Seamless Instant Switchover

When the grid goes down, your Solar Pro battery backup system automatically activates in milliseconds — so quickly your digital clocks won't even reset. 

- **Automatic Islanding:** Disconnects safely from the utility grid to power your home independently without sending electricity into downed power lines.
- **Whole-Home or Critical Loads:** Choose full-home backup or dedicated backup for critical circuits such as food storage, medical equipment, lighting, and HVAC.
- **Smart App Management:** Monitor battery percentage, solar generation, and household consumption in real-time from your smartphone.
